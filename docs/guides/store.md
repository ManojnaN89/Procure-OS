# Store guide

You receive the goods and say what is wrong with them.

## What you see when you log in

**Dashboard** → **My work**: **Orders with goods to come** and **Receipts waiting for inspection**.

## Receive goods

1. Open **Goods receipt**. The orders waiting for goods are listed.
2. Click **Receive goods** on the order that arrived.
3. For each line enter the quantity received, the quantity rejected and why. Add the delivery challan number and the supplier invoice number.
4. Click **Post receipt**. Stock goes up by the accepted quantity.

More than the tolerance allows over the order is refused. The tolerance is set per item category by the Admin.

## Inspect

Items marked for inspection wait. Open the receipt and click **Record inspection** with the result.

## Part deliveries

Receive what came. The order stays open for the rest. The buyer is reminded if the supplier's date passes.

## What you cannot do

You cannot enter the invoice or approve the order for the same goods. Someone else does those steps.

## If you are stuck

If a delivery has no order, ask the buyer to raise one. If something is wrong with a receipt you have already posted, tell the buyer and the AP clerk before the invoice is matched.
