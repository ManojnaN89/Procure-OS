# Store guide
You receive the goods and say what is wrong with them.
## What you see when you log in
**Dashboard** → **My work**: **Orders with goods to come** and **Receipts waiting for inspection**.
## Receive goods
1. Open **Goods receipt**. The orders waiting for goods are listed, the one whose first line is due soonest first. Each line shows its due date: the date the supplier promised, else the date it is needed ("needed"); a line past its date and not all received is marked late.
2. Click **Receive goods** on the order that arrived.
3. For each line enter the quantity received, the quantity rejected and why. Add the delivery challan number and the supplier invoice number. The person who asked for the goods and the buyer are told what arrived, and what was rejected.
4. Click **Post receipt**. Stock goes up by the accepted quantity.
Up to the tolerance (per item category, set by the Admin) is counted. What is above the order is not refused: it waits in quarantine, the receipt says "3 above the order held in quarantine; the buyer decides", and the buyer accepts it by revising the order or returns it. Rejected units go back with the delivery: the receipt makes one return challan (RTN) already dispatched, which you can see on the receipt and under **Quality hold and returns**. A part delivery leaves the order open for the rest. A laptop or other serialised item needs one serial number per unit; tick the rejected units' serials when you reject any, and a serial already received is refused.
When the supplier has recorded a dispatch, the order says "On the way": what was shipped, when, the vehicle or LR/AWB number and when it should arrive. You also get a notification. It is only what the supplier says: count what actually arrives.
## Inspect
Items whose item or category needs inspection wait. They are in the store as **quarantine** (Inventory shows "20 in quarantine"): never set aside or issued, and not yet received on the order. Open the receipt and click **Record inspection** with the result. The person who received the goods cannot inspect them (nor the buyer, an approver or the requester): someone else does, usually Quality. What passed becomes usable and is set aside at once; what was rejected stays **blocked** with the reason until it goes back; what is above the order stays in quarantine for the buyer.
## Return to a supplier
Open **Quality hold and returns**. It lists the blocked and excess holds. Tick the holds that go back, say how much of each and why, then **Make the draft return**. A hold you made by hand also says which receipt line the goods came from (opening or imported stock names only its supplier). Give the challan and vehicle and **Dispatch**: stock leaves the store, the hold closes, serials are freed, a counted return comes off the order line and its budget, and the supplier is mailed. A dispatched return is final; a draft can be **Cancel**. **Scrap** on a blocked hold writes off what cannot go back, with a reason.
## Reverse a receipt
Open the receipt and click **Reverse** with a reason. Inside the correction window (`grn_edit_window_days` working days on the place's calendar, three by default) it is done at once: the whole receipt goes back, stock leaves, reservations are given back, the line and budget are reduced, serials are reversed and the order returns to sent (or acknowledged). After the window the button asks the purchase manager, who approves it on Approvals. A receipt with an invoice matched, goods already issued, or a hold already returned or scrapped is refused: correct it with a return instead. An ERP receipt is reversed by the ERP's next import, not here.
## Issue stock and count the shelf
Open **Inventory**. On the row of the item click **Issue** to take stock out for use: enter the quantity and who it went to. You cannot issue more than the books show. After counting the shelf click **Count**: enter what you counted and why it differs. The system works out the difference and corrects the books. Count only usable stock: held lots are counted by their hold. Both are kept in the audit log with your name.
Safety stock is kept back: an issue that would use it is refused with "Only 2 is free; 2 is safety stock". Click **Ask to use safety stock** and say why; your manager approves it on My work, then the issue is posted.
**Hold** on an Inventory row puts usable stock in quarantine or blocked, with a reason. If that stock was set aside, the order needed last gives it back and the store check buys again for it. **Holds** lists the held lots: **Release** sends a quantity back to usable or, from quarantine, to blocked. Every hold and decision needs a reason.
## Material requests for production orders
The store check runs by itself every morning at 05:30 and after an order is closed, cancelled, changed or acknowledged, or a request is rejected or cancelled; the requests it makes wait as drafts unless the company turns on sending them for approval. **Run store check** does it now. A supply promised after the need-by (less the receiving buffer) does not count: the line shows "at risk 50" and the buyer gets a case. Each production order (or department's joiners, or MRP plan per plant) gets a material request; an MRP line marked "net" takes no free stock.
1. Open **Material requests** and click **Run store check**. Free stock is set aside for the orders needed soonest first; what is still short goes for approval as one purchase request per order.
2. When production starts, click **Issue for production**. Everything set aside is issued.
3. If the order is called off, click **Cancel** and say why; its stock goes to the next orders.
Changes from the ERP: **Load from the ERP** applies them and lists each change. If more was issued than is now needed you get a case on **Exceptions**: return the extra or keep it, then **Close** it.
## Goods with no order
Open **Receive without order**: enter the supplier, item, quantity, place and why, then **Record and ask the purchase manager**. Nothing goes into stock yet. Once the buyer raises the order and it is sent, click **Post the receipt**. If it is rejected, use the return form (challan and vehicle) and **Mark returned**: it makes one return document and mails the supplier. Services are not received here.
## What you cannot do, and if you are stuck
You cannot enter the invoice or approve the order for the same goods, and you cannot inspect what you received. **Exceptions** lists everything stuck that needs a person, oldest first, with the reason, who must act, the next step and the age in working days. If something is wrong with a receipt you have posted, reverse it inside the window, or tell the buyer and the AP clerk before the invoice is matched.
