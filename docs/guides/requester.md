# Requester guide

You ask for things. You see your own requests only.

## What you see when you log in

**Dashboard** → **My work** shows **My drafts** and **Returned to me for changes**.

## Ask for something

1. Open **Requests** and click **New request**. Choose the item, how many, when you need it and where it goes. Add a line for each item, and say why if you like.
2. Click **Save as draft**. The request opens, with a strip at the top showing where it is in the flow.
3. Check the lines and click **Submit for approval**. If it looks like a repeat of an open request, you are asked for a reason first.

You can also type what you need into the **Assistant**, and it drafts the request for you.

The system picks the approvers from the company's rules. You do not choose them.

## Follow it

**Requests** shows the status, and "waiting for" tells you who has it. If the approver is slow they get a reminder by themselves.

## If it comes back

An approver can **Return for changes**. It then shows under **Returned to me for changes**, with their reason. Fix it and click **Submit for approval** again.

## What you cannot do

You cannot approve your own request. You cannot see other people's requests.

## If you are stuck

Ask the **Assistant**: "where is my request?" If it is rejected, the reason is on the request.
