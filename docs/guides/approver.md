# Approver guide

You decide requests, orders and the sensitive things that no program may decide alone.

## What you see when you log in

**Dashboard** → **My work**. Sections you may see: **Requests waiting for my approval**, **Orders waiting for my approval**, **Payment runs to approve**, **Blocked invoices to release**, **Supplier changes to approve**, **Orders held for a price or ceiling**, **RFQs waiting to be sent**.

## Approve a request

1. Click the request in **My work**, or open **Requests**.
2. Read the lines, the amount and the budget warning if there is one.
3. Click **Approve**, **Return for changes** or **Reject**. Returning and rejecting need a reason: write it and click **Confirm**.
4. To approve less than was asked, click **Approve less…**, lower the quantities, write the reason and click **Approve with these quantities**. A quantity can go down, not up, and the value follows it.

Several at once: under **Requests**, open **Needs my decision**, tick the requests and click **Approve selected**. Each is decided on its own, and anything that is not your turn is left alone and listed.

By email: the mail about a request that waits for you shows the lines, the estimated total, the budget and why it came to you, with Approve, Reject and "Send back for changes" buttons. Each opens a page about the request; rejecting or sending back asks for a reason there. Above Rs 5,00,000, approving also needs a one-time code: click "Email me a code" on that page, type the six digits from the mail (it works once, for 10 minutes, 5 tries) and click Approve. The links work once and expire after a week. Opening one decides nothing.

If you do not decide, you get one reminder after a working day; after two working days the request also goes to your manager (or the next approver), who may then decide it.

## Approve an order

Orders are approved by tier, shown in the list of **Purchase orders**: the buyer up to Rs 1,00,000 (the buyer's issue is the approval), the purchase manager above that up to Rs 5,00,000, the director above Rs 5,00,000. Open **Purchase orders**, click the order, then **Approve** or **Reject**. You cannot approve an order you bought yourself. Orders marked **Touchless** were issued without a person, within the buyer's tier; the order page lists the facts that were checked. An order held for a price, a contract ceiling or a budget says why: the budget is checked again at the order's final price, and when the budget policy blocks, accepting it is your decision, under **Admin** → **Approvals**. An order over budget never approves itself, even when its total is within the automatic band.

## Decide the rest

Payment runs, releasing a blocked invoice, supplier changes and held orders are under **Admin** → **Approvals**. Click **Approve** or **Reject**. Nothing runs until you do.

## Check suppliers and contracts

**Vendors** → open a vendor → **Verify** a document after you compare it with the original. **Contracts** → open a draft → **Activate this contract**.

## What you cannot do

You cannot decide what you asked for, entered or proposed. You cannot verify a document you added. The buttons are not shown, and the server refuses.

## If you are stuck

If you will be away, open **Settings** (the gear at the top) → **Away? Name a deputy**, pick an approver and the days, and click **Name deputy**. Approvals that come up meanwhile go to them. To change who approves for good, ask the Admin (**Company setup** → **Approvers**).
