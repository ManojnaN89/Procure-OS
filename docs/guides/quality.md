# Quality guide
You inspect the goods the store receives, and decide what is good, what is blocked and what is above the order.
You are never the person who received the same goods.
## What you see when you log in
**Receiving** lists the orders with goods to come and the receipts already booked; **Receipts waiting for inspection** are on **My work**. **Quality hold and returns** shows the blocked and excess stock. **Inventory** shows every quality.
## Inspect a receipt
1. Open **Receiving** and the receipt waiting for inspection (the store tells you, and it is on **My work**).
2. For each line say how much passed and how much was rejected, with the reason. For a serialised item, tick the serial numbers of the rejected units: one per unit, from those received.
3. Click **Record inspection**.
What passed becomes usable at once and is set aside for the material requests that need it. What was rejected goes to **blocked** with your reason, waiting to go back or be scrapped. What is above what the order may still take stays in **quarantine** as excess: the buyer decides whether to accept it by revising the order or return it.
A receipt you received yourself cannot be inspected by you, nor by the order's buyer, an approver or the requester: the screen says so, and the company's readiness check (SEP-16) watches that the two duties have different people.
## Holds and decisions
On **Quality hold and returns** (or **Inventory**) a **Hold** puts usable stock in quarantine or blocked, with a reason: damage, expiry, a suspect lot. **Release** sends a quantity back to usable (set aside at once for the orders that need it) or, from quarantine, to blocked. Every decision needs a reason and is audited.
Blocked stock leaves only two ways:
- Return to a supplier: tick the hold on **Quality hold and returns**, say why, make the draft and dispatch it with the challan and vehicle. The supplier gets one mail.
- **Scrap**: what cannot go back is written off with a reason ("rusted, not returnable"); the value at the receipt's cost is shown.
## What you cannot do
You cannot receive receipts for the goods you inspect, approve an order or release its invoice, or enter the invoice. You cannot move goods out of blocked stock without a return or a scrap.
## If you are stuck
Ask the buyer for the excess (they accept it by revising the order or return it), and the store to dispatch a return. **Exceptions** lists the receipts waiting for an inspection and the draft returns, with how long each has waited.
